电子桌牌（TableCard）隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 15 日

电子桌牌重视你的隐私。本政策说明 TableCard 移动应用如何处理信息和系统权限。

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于电子桌牌（TableCard）移动应用。应用不要求你注册账户，不提供广告或用户行为分析功能，也不会将你的个人信息上传至开发者运营的互联网服务器。

2. 本地保存的信息

你添加的设备信息、人员名单及填写内容、模板、语言设置和导入字体等内容保存在设备本地应用存储中。这些内容仅用于实现应用功能，不用于建立用户画像。

3. 与附近设备的通信

当你主动添加设备、配置网络或投屏时，应用会通过蓝牙或本地 Wi-Fi 网络与所选电子桌牌或电子相框通信，并传输完成该操作所需的配置和展示内容。该等内容直接发送至你选择的附近设备，不会发送至开发者运营的互联网服务。

4. 权限和信息使用

  • 蓝牙和本地网络：用于发现、连接附近设备，以及向你选择的设备传输内容。
  • 照片：仅在你主动选择图片作为桌牌模板或电子相框内容时使用。
  • 文件：仅在你主动导入名单、备份或字体文件时读取所选文件。
  • 位置（部分 Android 版本）：仅用于满足 Android 10 及以下系统对蓝牙扫描的系统要求。

应用不请求或使用相机权限；当前版本不提供拍照或二维码扫码功能。应用只会在你主动使用相关功能时请求或使用相应权限。

5. 数据共享、广告与跟踪

应用不出售个人信息，不将个人信息用于广告，不会跨其他公司的应用或网站跟踪你。应用不包含广告 SDK 或分析 SDK，也不会向第三方出售或共享你的本地内容。

6. 保留、删除与撤回权限

本地内容会保留到你在应用中删除对应项目或卸载应用。你可以随时在系统设置中撤回照片、蓝牙、位置或本地网络等权限；撤回后，对应功能可能无法正常使用。

7. 联系我们

如对本政策、应用功能或数据处理有疑问，请通过电子邮件联系我们：tianqingwang@sina.com。

8. 政策更新

如应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面和应用内隐私政策，并更新页面顶部的最后更新日期。

TableCard Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

TableCard respects your privacy. This policy explains how the TableCard mobile app handles information and system permissions.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the TableCard mobile app. The app does not require an account, provide advertising or behavioral analytics, or upload your personal information to internet servers operated by the developer.

2. Information Stored Locally

Device information, member lists and content you enter, templates, language settings, and imported fonts are stored in local app storage on your device. This information is used only to provide app features and is not used to create a user profile.

3. Communication with Nearby Devices

When you add a device, configure a network, or cast content, the app communicates with the table card or photo frame you select over Bluetooth or a local Wi-Fi network. It transfers only the configuration and display content required for that action directly to the selected nearby device. This content is not sent to internet services operated by the developer.

4. Permissions and Information Use

  • Bluetooth and local network: used to discover and connect nearby devices and transfer content to devices you select.
  • Photos: used only when you choose an image as a table-card template or photo-frame item.
  • Files: used only when you choose a member list, backup, or font file to import.
  • Location on some Android versions: used only to meet Android system requirements for Bluetooth scanning on Android 10 and earlier.

The app does not request or use camera permission. The current version does not provide photo capture or QR-code scanning. Permissions are requested or used only when you choose the related feature.

5. Sharing, Advertising, and Tracking

The app does not sell personal information, use personal information for advertising, or track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell or share your local content with third parties.

6. Retention, Deletion, and Permission Withdrawal

Local content remains until you delete the relevant item in the app or uninstall the app. You may withdraw photo, Bluetooth, location, or local-network permissions in system settings at any time; related features may then stop working.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, app features, or the app's data handling, contact us at tianqingwang@sina.com.

8. Policy Updates

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the in-app privacy policy and revise the last-updated date above.
